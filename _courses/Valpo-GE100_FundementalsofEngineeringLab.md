## Student Learning Objectives
After successfully completing this course, students will be able to:
1. Explain the essence of engineering and distinguish between different engineering disciplines and subdisciplines.
2. Use laboratory instrumentation to measure physical quantities.
3. Use measured or simulated data to verify and/or apply fundamental engineering concepts.
4. Represent data graphically and verbally.
5. Demonstrate applying computational reasoning.
6. Distinguish between engineering analysis and engineering design.
7. Describe and apply an engineering design process.
8. Demonstrate professional behaviors and practices.
9. Describe habits for academic success.
10. Demonstrate group and individual accountability in team projects.
